Each change to a machine goes through the same gate.
A controller proposes a [](kind:HostOperation), a person approves it, and only then the executor runs it.
[](concept:approval) explains why.

<ApprovalFlow />

## Steps

1. A controller proposes a HostOperation. Its phase is `AwaitingApproval`.
2. Find the operations that wait with [](cli:status) or [](cli:top).
3. Read the plan and approve it with [](cli:approve). You can approve your own proposal only if your site allows it. See [](howto:approval-permissions).
4. The [](ctrl:hostoperation) checks the [gates before the start](kind:HostOperation#gates-before-the-start).
5. The executor runs the actions one at a time. See [](act:index).
6. The operation ends as `Succeeded`, `Failed` or `Aborted`.

## Your commands

| You want to | Command | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See what waits | [](cli:status) | Lists operations in `AwaitingApproval` |
| Approve | [](cli:approve) | Writes `spec.approval.approvedBy` with your name |
| Stop new proposals for a claim | [](cli:hold) | Sets `metal.platform-kit.org/hold` on the claim |
| Allow proposals again | [](cli:unhold) | Removes the hold |
| Stop an approved operation | [](cli:abort) | Stops before the next action and undoes media, boot override and cordon |
| Try a failed operation again | [](cli:retry) | Deletes it, the operator proposes it again |

<Callout type="note">
If you delete a proposed operation, the operator proposes the same plan again.
To say no, hold the claim or remove `spec.os` from the claim.
</Callout>

## Who may do what

[The roles](howto:approval-permissions#the-roles) tells who proposes, who approves and who is in the admin group.

## Rollouts

A [](kind:TalosRollout) has one approval for the full plan.
The operations that it makes need no approval of their own. See [](flow:day-2-change).
