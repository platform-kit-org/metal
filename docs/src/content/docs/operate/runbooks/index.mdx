Start with [](cli:status). Find the value from the `PHASE` or `BLOCKED` column, or the reason of a condition, in the table.
Each runbook has the same parts: symptoms, impact, diagnosis, fix and verification.

## Alerts

Each alert links to one runbook in its `runbook_url` annotation.

| Alert | Fires when | Runbook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PkMetalMetricsAbsent` | Prometheus did not scrape the manager, for 10 minutes | [](rb:management-cluster-lost) |
| `PkMetalAdmissionGateDegraded` | An admission policy of the approval gate, or its binding, is missing or does not deny, for 2 minutes | [](rb:admission-gate-degraded) |
| `PkMetalReconcileFailing` | More than half of the passes of one controller fail, for 30 minutes | [](rb:reconcile-failing) |
| `PkMetalReconcilePanicked` | A controller panicked in the last hour | [](rb:reconcile-failing) |
| `PkMetalOperationWorkersBusy` | Each operation worker is busy, for 1 hour | [](rb:workers-busy) |
| `PkMetalOperationFailed` | An operation failed, for 5 minutes | [](rb:operation-failed) |
| `PkMetalOperationStuck` | An operation is in one step for more than 2 hours | [](rb:operation-stuck) |
| `PkMetalOperationBlocked` | An approved operation did not start for 1 hour, and no window or queue holds it | [](rb:operation-held) |
| `PkMetalApprovalWaiting` | An operation waits more than one day for approval | [](rb:operation-held) |
| `PkMetalBMCUnreachable` | A BMC does not answer, for 15 minutes | [](rb:bmc-unreachable) |
| `PkMetalNodeNotReady` | A node that answered before is not `Ready`, for 15 minutes | [](rb:node-not-ready) |
| `PkMetalClusterUnavailable` | A bootstrapped cluster is not available, for 15 minutes | [](rb:cluster-unavailable) |
| `PkMetalEtcdBackupStale` | The newest verified backup is older than two backup intervals | [](rb:backup-failing) |
| `PkMetalEtcdBackupMissing` | A backup policy has no verified snapshot after 1 hour | [](rb:backup-failing) |
| `PkMetalCredentialsExpiring` | A published credential has less than one sixth of its lifetime left | [](rb:credentials-not-renewed) |
| `PkMetalRolloutStalled` | A rollout waits for health, cannot plan, or waits after its first node, for 1 hour | [](rb:rollout-paused) |
| `PkMetalRolloutFailed` | A node of a rollout failed, or its plan changed and no later rollout replaces it, for 5 minutes | [](rb:rollout-paused) |

## Before you change anything

Follow [](howto:collect-diagnostics). The facts help if the fix does not work.

## After an incident

To find who proposed, approved and lifted a guard on each change, read the [](ref:audit-trail). A finished operation stays for the site retention, 30 days by default. The audit logs stay longer.
